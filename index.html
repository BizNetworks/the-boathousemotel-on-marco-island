Welcome

A Relaxed Place to Stay on Marco Island

Planning a trip and comparing hotels on Marco Island beach? The Boat House Motel offers a calm, friendly and affordable base close to the Gulf, with the shops and restaurants of Old Marco within walking distance. Guests return year after year for the quiet setting and the genuine welcome.

Our family-run property sits on the banks of Collier Creek. Spend the morning with coffee on the dock watching boats, birds and dolphins, spend the afternoon on the sand, and end the day with a sunset from the water's edge.

Whether you want a weekend getaway, a fishing trip, a boating stopover or a long winter stay, you will find clean rooms, soft towels and staff who treat you like family.

Beach Life

Marco Island's soft white beaches are famous for shelling, calm water and relaxed days.

Boating & Fishing

Fish for snook, redfish and tarpon, or tour the Ten Thousand Islands by boat.

Dining

Shopping

Wildlife & Nature

Entertainment

Boaters & Anglers

Arrive by Boat, Fish From the Dock

Our docks welcome guests who arrive by water, and a few boat slips let you tie up steps from your room. Prefer to stay on land? Toss a line from the dock and try your luck while the sun sets.

Local captains offer inshore and offshore charters, and nearby ramps and marinas make it easy to explore Southwest Florida's waters. Ask our team for suggestions when you arrive.
